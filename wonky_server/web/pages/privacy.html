Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wonky Work (Wonky Write and Wonky Sheets, “the service”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The service is provided by Serverpod AB, which is the data controller for the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier.
  • Your content: the documents, spreadsheets, comments, files and version history you create or upload, and who you share them with.
  • Preferences: settings such as theme, language and notification choices.
  • Technical data: log data such as IP address, browser type, timestamps and error reports, used to run and secure the service.
  • Usage data: on which days you used which Wonky Work app (or an MCP client with your access token), whether you created or edited something that day, and when you were last seen. We keep it for up to 400 days. It never includes what is in your files.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: storing and syncing your files, real-time collaboration, sharing, offline access and notifications (including emails you can turn off).
  • To keep the service secure and reliable, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To understand how the service is used, as aggregate statistics for our team (for example how many people use each app), and to improve it.
  • To respond to your requests and support questions.

We do not sell your personal data, show ads or use tracking cookies. The legal bases we rely on are performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running a secure service, and your consent where we ask for it.

AI features

AI features are optional. When you use one, the content needed for the request (for example your prompt and the relevant part of a document) is sent to the AI provider in use (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) to produce the answer. If you add your own API key, your requests are sent to that provider under your own account with them; the key is stored on our servers only to make those requests and is never shown back to you or anyone else. We do not use your content to train AI models.

Who we share it with

  • People you choose to share files with, and collaborators on shared files.
  • Service providers that help us run the service, such as hosting, email delivery and (when you use AI features) AI providers. They may only process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, where we are required to by law.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our hosting providers. Where service providers process data outside the EU/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Cookies and local storage

We only use what the service needs to work: cookies or browser storage that keep you signed in, remember the app you used last and your settings, and copies of files you make available offline.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Files you move to the trash are deleted permanently after 30 days. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and content within 30 days, except where we must keep something by law or until it is removed from backups.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data, and object to or restrict how we process it. Most of this you can do in the app (you can download your files from the app and delete your account in Settings); for anything else, contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Children

The service is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above, and we tell you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
